# Activity Outcomes — Group 3: The Food Waster (*FoodLoop*)

---

## 1. Product Concept
**FoodLoop** is a product idea which allows university students to limit food wastage and facilitates exchange of extra or unrequired food, in order to provide students with cheap alternatives. App functionality would include: items being digitally recorded (by image, barcode and/or typed information); notification of consumption and/or food wastage; expiry and/or disuse alerts; recipe creation using existing items; and advising students on how much to buy based on their previous buying and wastage behaviours. People would also be able to exchange, and if required, commercially trade, excess food items on a campus marketplace.

## 2. Product Name
**FoodLoop** —, to show that food is continuously cycled through the system: from its point of purchase to when it is either consumed or exchanged, and therefore minimising food going to waste

## 3. Target User
* **Primary Users:** Students who both purchase and/or keep food and/or prepared food. Students may neglect, excessively acquire or dispose of food as a result of their hectic lifestyle and/or lack of knowledge regarding how to utilise particular food items.

## 4. Key Features (Conceptual Design)
* **Item Recording & Monitoring: Items can be documented within the app by image, barcode and/or typing. Users can then amend the quantity of recorded food as it is consumed and/or wasted.
* **Disuse & Expiry Alerts: Notifications are sent to inform students that particular food items in storage have not recently been used and/or are nearing their expiry date.
* **AI Recipe Advisor: Large Language Modelling technology is used to recommend realistic recipes by analysing and utilising a person’s current food stock.
* **Shopping Advice (Random Forest Regressor): Through analysis of a student’s past purchasing, consumption and wastage behaviour (for instance, recommending that 6-7 rather than 10 bananas should be purchased next time, having realised that on a previous occasion 6 were consumed and 4 were subsequently wasted), this feature advises people on future shopping amounts.
* **Food Exchange / Campus Marketplace: An imaginary platform for students to exchange and/or commercially trade appropriate additional food at cost-effective rates, so that extra food can be obtained by those who can make use of it.
